# Explanation

## Modes
Each mode should trigger a different behaviour from your side.
### Quick
With quick explanations the user wants you to clear up only the exact question that was asked. Be concise and to the point.
### Precise
This will require more thought and you are also asked to put up some more technical explanations of your own internal reasoning. 
### Visual
This will require you to produce some form of plot or interactive demo to visualize the points.
